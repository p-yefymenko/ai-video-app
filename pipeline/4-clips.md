# 4. Clips

For every shot in `epNN/shots.md` that has no `epNN/<shot>.mp4`, generate a video and save it there.

- **References:** `refs/<id>.png` for each character in the shot and for its place. Use them as references, not as a start frame.
- **Prompt:** the show's Style, the place's fixed details, the shot's Action, and its Dialogue. After each speaker's name, add that character's Voice from `show.md`, word for word. End with: "Characters look at each other, never at the camera."
- **Format:** vertical 9:16, 720p, the shot's Length, with audio.

Watch every clip from start to end. Make it again if anyone looks at or talks to the camera, or if anything is physically wrong: a body merging into the floor or furniture, a person vanishing or appearing out of nowhere.
